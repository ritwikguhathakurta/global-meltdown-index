# Outreach tracker — Global Meltdown Index

A log for every real-world outreach action (a community post, a DM, an email to a newsletter
curator) once one actually happens. **Nothing in this file has happened yet** — every row below is
a planned candidate, not a completed action, and none of them get executed without your explicit
go-ahead at the time, not a blanket approval of this document.

## Ground rules (apply to every row, no exceptions)

1. **No automation.** No bot, script, or scheduled job ever posts or messages on this project's
   behalf. A human (you) reads the final text and clicks "submit," every time.
2. **No deception.** Always post as yourself, disclosing you're the author/maintainer. Never use a
   sockpuppet or a "neutral third party" framing to make the project look more organically
   discovered than it is.
3. **No vote/engagement manipulation.** No asking for upvotes, no alt accounts, no "engagement pods,"
   no buying followers/stars — several of the researched communities explicitly forbid this, and
   it's against this project's own no-fabrication discipline regardless of what's allowed.
4. **Re-verify the community's current rules immediately before posting**, not from
   `docs/growth/COMMUNITY_RESEARCH.md` alone — that doc is dated 2026-10-22 and most of its entries
   are secondhand summaries, not the platform's own current rules page.
5. **One outreach action per community per cadence** — no re-posting the same link to the same
   place repeatedly hoping it lands; that's the spam behavior every community rule above forbids.
6. **Log the outcome honestly**, including "ignored" or "removed by moderators" — this tracker is
   for learning what actually works, not a highlight reel.

## What to fill in when something actually happens

- **Date contacted** — the real date, not the planned date.
- **Outcome** — upvotes/comments/stars gained (cite the actual number from
  `docs/growth/METRICS.md`'s next snapshot, not a guess), or "removed by moderators," "no response,"
  etc.
- **Notes** — anything learned for next time (e.g. "title was too salesy, got flagged").

## Explicitly not in scope for this tracker

- Mass/bulk outreach to lists of people — this project's growth plan explicitly excludes outreach
  automation and anything that manufactures activity.
- Any outreach to a specific named individual who hasn't been identified yet as a real, relevant
  contact for a real reason (e.g. "this person wrote about a similar project last month") — adding
  a row for a person speculatively, with no real hook, is the kind of low-effort outreach the
  ground rules above exist to prevent.
