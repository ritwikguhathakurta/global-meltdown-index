# Product Hunt draft

Secondhand research only (official PH help pages not retrievable this pass) — self-hunting your
own launch is reportedly fine; directly asking for upvotes is against policy, ask for feedback
instead. See `docs/growth/COMMUNITY_RESEARCH.md`. Also check: PH guidance suggests a maker account
with some age/history does better than a brand-new one — plan the timing accordingly.

**Tagline** (under ~60 characters):
> Free-data systemic risk terminal, every number traceable

**Description:**
> Global Meltdown Index turns only free, official macro-financial data into 24 independently-scored
> risk engines and one composite score — recomputed daily, with every stale or missing number
> labeled as such instead of guessed at. No signup needed to check it's real: the public API
> returns live data right now. Not a forecast — a current risk signal, clearly labeled as one.
> Apache-2.0 licensed.

**First comment (maker's own, framed as feedback-seeking, not vote-seeking):**
> Built this over ~7 weeks with one rule: every number real and traceable, or the UI says so
> honestly. Would love feedback — especially from anyone who's evaluated a "risk score" product
> before and knows what actually earns trust vs. what's just noise.
