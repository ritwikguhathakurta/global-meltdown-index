# Outreach email template

**This is a template, not a ready-to-send email.** The master plan's own rule: email outreach must
be "brief, personalised, relevant, and sent only to suitable contacts with appropriate consent." No
recipient has been identified yet — `docs/growth/DISTRIBUTION_PLAYBOOK.md`'s lead-research table is
currently empty. Do not fill in a name and send this until there's a real, specific, relevant
recipient and a real reason tied to something they've actually published or built.

> Subject: [Specific reason tied to their work — e.g. "Following up on your piece on X"]

>
> Live: https://globalmeltdownindex.net · Code (Apache-2.0):
> https://github.com/ritwikguhathakurta/global-meltdown-index
>
> [Your name]

## Rules for using this

- Fill in every bracket with something true and specific, or don't send it.
- One email per real recipient — never a mail-merge blast.
- If they don't respond, that's the answer. No automated follow-up sequence.
- Respect whatever their own "contact me" page says about how they want to be approached.
