# 30-day KPI plan

The actual tracking mechanics, real Day-0 baseline, and the full "what not to do with these
numbers" guidance live in `docs/growth/METRICS.md` — this file is the thin, launch-specific layer
on top of it: which week to check what, tied to `docs/growth/CONTENT_CALENDAR.md`'s schedule.

## Checkpoints

| Week | What to check | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Baseline already captured 2026-10-22 — see `METRICS.md` | Nothing to compare against later without this |
| End of Week 1 | Stars/forks/watchers unchanged expectation (nothing public posted yet) | Confirms Week 1 activity (if any) didn't leak attention prematurely |
| End of Week 2 | Referrer data after blog post #1 + first HN/community shares | First real signal of whether *content* (not just a launch announcement) drives traffic |
| End of Week 3 | Referrer data after Show HN / Product Hunt / Reddit | The highest-single-day-impact checkpoint — expect the most informative referrer breakdown here |
| Day 30 | Full baseline-to-Day-30 comparison | Closes the loop — see `METRICS.md`'s weekly table |

## The one rule that matters most here

Don't set a numeric target (e.g. "100 stars by Day 30") — `METRICS.md` explains why: there's a true
zero baseline and nothing comparable to derive a target from. Track direction, and attribute a
change to a specific action only when the referrer data actually confirms it.
